P.O.G.O.

P.O.G.O. (Poly Output Gravity Object) is an endless runner mobile game, which uses the gyroscope and microphone in its gameplay.

Project Innovation

P.O.G.O. is the result of project Innovation, a project in the second year of Creative Media & Game Technologies. The goal was to create an innovative application, that would use different inputs than regular touch or keyboard input. The project took 3 weeks, and I worked in a team of 6 people. P.O.G.O. uses the gyroscope to move forward, and the microphone input to 'blow away' the cloud obstacles.

I focused mostly on perfecting the in-game movement based on the gyroscope input from the phone. I also programmed various game mechanics like variable difficulty waves and collectibles.

Tools and languages
